LinkHarvest is a link extractor for Chrome and Edge that pulls every hyperlink out of the page you are on and turns the result into a list you can work with. Scan once, then search, filter, sort, copy or export to TXT, CSV or JSON. An optional Link Audit checks the status of those links and shows which ones are broken, blocked or redirected. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Everything it does

The core features at a glance.

01

One-click scan of every link on the current page, up to 10,000 per scan.

02

Real-time monitor catches links added after the page loads, which matters on infinite scroll and single-page apps.

03

Search by link text or address, with separate include and exclude word filters.

04

Hide anchor links, script pseudo-links and protocol links such as mailto and tel with one switch each.

05

Occurrence count shows how many times each address appears on the page.

06

Copy the filtered list as plain URLs, Markdown, HTML or JSON Lines.

Also included

  • Open the filtered links in background tabs, with an adjustable delay between them.
  • Export TXT, JSON or CSV; the CSV is UTF-8 with a byte-order mark, so Excel and WPS open it cleanly.
  • Link Audit for status codes, redirect targets and response times.
  • Right-click a page to extract its links, or press Alt+Shift+L.
  • Results page with a full table, row selection and paging.
  • Dark mode follows your system theme.

02

Free vs Premium

The free tier is a complete tool, not a trial. Premium is optional.

Free

scanning, the real-time monitor, search, filters, saved presets, sorting, multi-format copy, opening links in tabs, TXT, JSON and CSV export, dark mode, and a Link Audit preview of 100 links per run.

Premium

Link Audit up to 2,000 links per run and the CSV audit report. One key activates on two browsers, and removing the licence from one browser frees that slot again.

No licence is needed to extract, copy or export links, and every free feature works without an account. Premium asks the licence server for a check about every two hours. It keeps working while you are offline; a failed check never switches the paid features off.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Read exactly what is stored and when the extension uses the network.
